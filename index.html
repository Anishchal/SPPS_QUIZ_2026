📋 SPPQ Rules & Mark Scheme

  • Total Marks: 50 Marks across 10 randomized questions:
    • Q1 – Q4 (Easy): 3 marks each (12 marks)
    • Q5 – Q7 (Medium): 5 marks each (15 marks)
    • Q8 & Q9 (Hard): 7 marks each (14 marks)
    • Q10 (Hard): 9 marks (9 marks)
  • Time Limit: You have strictly 30 minutes to complete the quiz once started.
  • Do Not Reload: Avoid refreshing or closing the page. Your progress will be tracked, but reloading risks accidental submission if time runs out.
  • Auto-Submission: If the 30-minute timer runs out, the quiz will automatically submit with any unanswered questions recorded as blank (0 marks).
  • Single Attempt: Once you move past a question, you cannot return to edit your choice.
  • Certificate: Upon quiz completion, your score is logged and your Certificate of Participation will be generated.
